Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy covers the ToteTag app for iPhone, iPad and Android (“the app”).

We don’t collect your data

The developer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no servers.

What stays on your device

Your totes, their names and locations, the items in them and any photos you add are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them from the device.

iCloud sync and sharing

If you're signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy of your totes and photos in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they stay in sync across your devices. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see it.

If you invite people with Family Sharing, the people you invite can see and change the shared totes, and they see your name as the owner. You can stop sharing or remove people at any time from Family Sharing in the app.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan tote labels and to take item photos. Photos you take or choose are saved inside the app (and in your iCloud if sync is on). Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Siri

When you ask Siri where something is, the app searches your totes on your device to answer.

Android

On Android, your totes, items and photos are stored only on your phone. There’s no iCloud sync, family sharing or Siri. If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include this data in your own Google account backup, which the developer can’t see. Scanning uses Google’s code scanner, part of Google Play services. The camera picture is read on your phone, and Google Play services may send Google basic diagnostic information (such as the device model and app version) under Google’s privacy policy. The developer receives none of it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
